## About Afyra

Afyra is a crop-residue sourcing and collection platform connecting farmers with animal feed manufacturers. It helps farmers make agricultural residues available to buyers while supporting quality verification, supply matching, pooling and collection coordination.

The platform combines mobile and web applications, backend services and AI-assisted quality screening to support the movement of agricultural residues from farms to manufacturers.

## How Afyra Works

From agricultural residues to coordinated supply.

<div class="afyra-steps">
<div class="afyra-step">

**01: List Crop Residues**

Farmers submit available crop residues with their quantity, location and supporting photographs.

</div>
<div class="afyra-step">

**02: Verify Quality**

AI-assisted image classification helps assess the quality of submitted crop residues.

</div>
<div class="afyra-step">

**03: Match & Pool Supply**

The platform matches eligible listings with manufacturers' requirements and groups supply for collection.

</div>
<div class="afyra-step">

**04: Coordinate Collection**

Farmers, manufacturers and drivers coordinate pickup and collection through the platform.

</div>
</div>
